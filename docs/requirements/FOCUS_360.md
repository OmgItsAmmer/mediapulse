# Social Media Monitoring System

### **Features**

* **Keyword and Hashtag Monitoring:** Tracks predefined keywords, hashtags, and their variations. Provides real-time alerts for spikes in activity.
* **Sentiment Analysis:** Utilizes AI-powered NLP to classify content sentiment as positive, negative, or neutral.
* **Engagement Metrics:** Tracks interactions like shares, comments, likes, views, and impressions to measure campaign effectiveness.
* **Trend Identification:** Detects emerging discussions, viral content, and changing public preferences.
* **Advertisement Tracking:** Monitors reach, impressions, engagement rates, and ROI for social media, print, and electronic ads.
* **Influencer Campaign Analysis:** Evaluates influencer collaborations based on ROI, engagement, and audience demographics.
* **Visual Dashboard:** Real-time web-based interface with customizable filters, visual charts, and heatmaps.
* **Data Management & Search:** Employs relational databases, data lakes, and Elasticsearch for organizing, indexing, and retrieving media and metadata.
* **Metadata Tagging:** Automated tagging for sentiment, keywords, and topics, alongside manual annotation options.
* **User Management (RBAC):** Role-based access control, multi-factor authentication (MFA), SSO integration, and comprehensive activity logs.
* **Clipping Module:** Manual and automated video clip creation, searchable clip library, and export/sharing options.
* **Speech-to-Text Conversion:** AI-powered transcription of live or recorded electronic media broadcasts across multiple languages.
* **Print Media Monitoring (OCR):** Extracts text from scanned/digital newspapers for keyword matching and thematic categorization.
* **Automated Reporting:** Generates diverse reports including Engagement, Sentiment Analysis, Competitor Analysis, Trend Analysis, Crisis Management, and Executive Summaries.

### **Tech Stack**

**Frontend / UI**
* **Frameworks:** React, Angular, or Vue.js. 
* **Templates:** Paid React.js/Next.js themes for dashboards.
* **Design Tools:** Figma Pro, Adobe XD.

**Backend / API**
* **Languages & Frameworks:** Python (Flask, Django, FastAPI), Node.js.

**Machine Learning & AI**
* **Computer Vision:** OpenCV, TensorFlow, PyTorch.
* **OCR:** Tesseract, EasyOCR, LAMMA OCR API.
* **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** spaCy, NLTK, Hugging Face Transformers.
* **Large Language Models (LLMs):** GPT API, OpenAI, LangChain.

**Data Collection (Crawlers)**
* **Libraries:** Python (Scrapy, BeautifulSoup, Selenium).
* **Proxy/Networking:** Zyte (Crawlera) for IP rotation, Proton VPN for bypassing restrictions.

**Data Processing & Analytics**
* **Data Handling:** Pandas, NumPy, SQL.
* **Data Streaming:** Apache Kafka (Confluent Kafka).
* **Visualization:** Tableau, Power BI.

**Infrastructure & DevOps**
* **Cloud Platforms:** AWS, Azure, GCP.
* **Containerization & Orchestration:** Docker, Kubernetes.
* **Virtualization:** VMware.
* **CI/CD:** Jenkins.

**Quality Assurance**
* **Testing Tools:** Selenium, JUnit.
